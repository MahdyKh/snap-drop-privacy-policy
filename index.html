1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Snap & Drop handles information. Snap & Drop is published by Next Studio ("we", "us", "our"). It is a single-player photo puzzle game for Android.

We wrote this policy to describe what the game actually does. We do not run our own servers for the game, we do not offer user accounts, and we do not receive your game progress. The game shows advertising provided by Google. Google collects certain device and usage information to deliver those ads, as described below.

2. Scope of this policy

This policy applies to the Snap & Drop Android app (com.snapdrop.game) and to this privacy policy page. It does not apply to third-party services, websites or ads that you may reach from the game. Those are governed by their own privacy policies.

3. Information stored locally by the game

The game saves the following information in its private storage on your device:

  • game progress, such as completed levels and stars, coins, hints and undos, collected pictures, unlocked colour themes, daily-challenge streak, statistics and achievements;
  • settings, such as music, sound and vibration on or off;
  • a counter the game uses to space out full-screen ads.

This information does not include your name, email address, phone number, precise location or any account identifier. The game does not send this information to us or to anyone else.

If Android backup is enabled on your device, Android may include this saved game data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is handled by Android and Google, not by us.

4. Advertising and the Google Mobile Ads SDK

Snap & Drop uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to show ads. The game shows:

  • a banner ad on menu screens, but not on the puzzle board during play;
  • an occasional full-screen ad between game sessions, never in the middle of a puzzle;
  • a rewarded ad, but only when you choose to watch one in exchange for free hints.

According to Google's published data disclosure for this SDK, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares the following information for advertising, analytics and fraud-prevention purposes:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate the general (approximate) location of the device;
  • product interaction information, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app and SDK performance (for example launch time, hang rate and energy use);
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

Google states that this information is encrypted in transit. Ads are not required to play. If ads cannot load, for example when the device is offline, the game continues to work.

5. Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) and consent

Before requesting ads, the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to check whether a consent message is required where you live. This applies, for example, in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. If a message is required, it is shown so that you can make your choices about how your data is used for advertising.

Where Google requires a way to change your choice later, the game shows a Privacy choices button in its Settings screen. You can use it to review or change your consent at any time. The types of ads you see (for example personalised or non-personalised ads) depend on your choices and on Google's rules for your region.

6. Advertising identifiers and device information

The Android advertising ID is a resettable identifier provided by Google Play services. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may use it, together with device information such as device model, operating system version, language and network information, to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Snap & Drop itself does not read, store or link the advertising ID with any other identifier.

The app declares the following Android permissions:

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and consent messages. Gameplay itself does not need a connection.
  • Advertising ID and Android ad services (ad ID, attribution, topics): declared by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for ads and ad measurement.
  • Vibrate: for optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in the game's Settings.
  • Wake lock and foreground service: added by Google Play services libraries that the ads SDK depends on. The game does not use them itself.

The game does not request access to your precise location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files, phone or SMS.

7. Third-party service providers

The game includes the following third-party software:

  • Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob), provided by Google LLC, to show ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), provided by Google LLC, to request and store consent choices for ads.
  • Unity engine, provided by Unity Technologies, which the game is built with. Unity Analytics, Unity crash reporting and Unity performance reporting are not enabled for this game. The Unity engine may still send limited technical information about the device (such as device model, operating system and graphics hardware) to Unity Technologies, as described in Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

8. Data sharing and processing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not receive or share your game data, because the game keeps it only on your device.

The information described in sections 4 to 7 is collected directly by the third-party providers named above, through their software in the game. They process it under their own privacy policies. Google may share advertising data with its advertising partners as described in its policies and in accordance with your consent choices.

9. Data retention

  • On your device: your game data stays until you uninstall the game or clear its storage.
  • With us: we do not collect or store your game data or advertising data on our own systems.
  • With third parties: information collected by Google or Unity is kept according to their own retention policies, which we do not control.

If you contact us by email, we keep your message and email address only as long as needed to answer you and to keep a record of the request.

10. Data security

Your game data is stored in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on a standard Android device. Google states that the information collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we choose to keep your game data on your device rather than collecting it.

11. Children's privacy

Snap & Drop is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the game itself does not ask for any personal information. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has used the game and you have a privacy question, please contact us at the email address below.

12. Your privacy rights and choices

  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. On most Android devices this is under Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads. The wording can vary by device.
  • Consent choices: where available, use Settings → Privacy choices in the game to review or change your consent for ads.
  • Delete your game data: uninstall the game, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Snap & Drop → Storage → Clear storage. This permanently deletes your progress from the device. We hold no account or game data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete.
  • Google's controls: for information collected by Google, you can use Google's tools at https://myadcenter.google.com and the options in Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Other rights: depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal information. Because we do not hold personal information about you through the game, such requests about advertising data are best directed to Google. You may also contact us with any question and we will help where we can.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy, for example if the game adds a new feature or third-party service. The updated policy will be published on this page with a new effective date. Please review it from time to time.
